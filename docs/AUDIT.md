# CaldaSpace senior product audit

Date: 2026-06-18

## Verdict

Family Hub 1.0 had a distinctive visual identity, a strong five-section information architecture and an effective mobile navigation pattern. Its weakness was product depth: limited continuity, shallow learning, inconsistent provenance, fragile external-data states and incomplete accessibility made it an elegant prototype rather than a dependable family product.

## What worked

- Original dark cosmic identity and orbital motif
- Clear Home, Discover, Learn, Observe and Play structure
- Effective mobile bottom navigation
- Explicit Live, Historical and Simulated labels
- Low-friction access without accounts

## What needed improvement

- Stronger repeat-use and family progression loops
- Multiple profiles and saved discoveries
- Consistent source, freshness and evidence metadata
- Better empty, loading, offline and retry states
- Full keyboard, focus and target-size accessibility
- Separate explanation depths for children and adults
- Modular, repeatably tested release packaging

## Usefulness

CaldaSpace is useful as a family space discovery ritual: a trusted daily image, current orbital information, visual search, understandable science and short shared challenges. It is not positioned as a professional observation or research-planning tool.
